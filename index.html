Matas Pajarskas

artist name:

Pajja

Drawing inspiration from nature and movement, Pajja’s sound invites listeners to explore and reflect. His artistic practice spans both production and DJing, with his latest album ‘altering’ being an exploration of feelings and experiences that lie beyond language or reason. Lithuanian-born, Amsterdam-based, Pajja works to create a liberating atmosphere where listeners can fully immerse themselves in the freedoms of sound. Dedicated to experimentation, his DJ sets seamlessly move between house, techno, trance and breaks, guiding dancers to a space for emotion and expression in movement. Pajja’s ambient sets remain his truest passion, with a focus on relaxing, meditative and obscure sounds, offering space to slow down and drift.
